🛡️ Tourist Safety Tips

Follow these tips to stay safe while traveling.

📍 Share Your Location

Share your live location with trusted family or friends.

📞 Save Emergency Numbers

Keep local police, ambulance, and emergency contacts saved on your phone.

💼 Protect Your Valuables

Keep passports, wallets, and important documents in a secure place.

🌙 Avoid Isolated Areas

Travel through well-lit and populated places, especially at night.

🚕 Use Trusted Transport

Choose registered taxis or verified ride-sharing services whenever possible.

📱 Keep Your Phone Charged

Carry a power bank so you can make emergency calls if needed.

🏥 Know Nearby Hospitals

Locate nearby hospitals and medical centers before traveling.

🚨 Report Incidents

Use the Report Incident feature immediately if you face any emergency.
